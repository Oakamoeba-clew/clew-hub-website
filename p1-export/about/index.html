About

CLEW Industries

Commercial OEMs, defense integrators, and primes buying build-to-print. Lehigh Valley, Pennsylvania.

CLEW Industries designs mechanical interface hardware — plates, adapters, and the files that make them usable across platforms.

The company exists so an integrator can mount a payload to a published pattern instead of starting from a custom drill template on every host.

It is run with a supply-chain and traceability background: revision control, configuration discipline, and a file a buyer can put in a technical data package. Not a marketing story.

CLEW also takes the work as a subcontractor. Send the print, the revision, the quantity, and the inspection the order requires. Customer-furnished data stays yours.

Hardware is sourced through a U.S. manufacturing network. Drawings are revision-controlled. Inspection and a certificate of conformance ship when the order calls for them. First article when required.

Hardware offered for lawful commercial and government-integrator use. Buyer is responsible for export classification and end-use compliance.
