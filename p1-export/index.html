How it works

From a requirement
to a part.

A short path from a drawing or a requirement to a quoted part.

  1. 01

    Select or describe

    Choose P1, or describe the hardware you need.

  2. 02

    Review and quote

    We confirm the design, then send a quote and a timeline.

  3. 03

    Source and manufacture

    Sourced through trusted U.S. partners, then inspected and documented to the order.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Parts documented to the order, ready to install.

Start with
P1.

P1 is the published interface. Geometry that repeats can become another CLEW standard. New requirements are quoted as custom hardware.

Describe the requirement

  • Low quantities

    Quantities are quoted. There is no catalog minimum.

  • Materials

    6061-T6 aluminum, stainless, and the finish called on the order.

  • Specified to the order

    Material, finish, and inspection follow the host and the environment.

  • U.S. sourcing

    Sourced through trusted U.S. partners.
