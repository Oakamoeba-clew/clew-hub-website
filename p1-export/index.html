02Maritime

P1-M

Dry payload-bay kit. For unmanned surface craft and marine electronics enclosures.

The face goes on a dry bulkhead. The harness is quoted to your payload ICD. Default if unnamed: Micro-D 21 (M83513/04-C11N or equal). Length from your bay dims. No cut until pinout is signed. You own the payload ICD and the hull or bay dims. CLEW owns the kit ICD.

Each kit includes the components, documentation, and serial labels required for first-article verification.

Not includedExcludes hull redesign, high-power, wet-mate, on-site labor.

P1-A

Payload-face swap pack. Mechanical only. Field replace.
